    🧠 Mindset

Tips

  • Deconstruct the skill:Break the hard topic down into smaller, essential building blocks using the 80/20 rule to focus on what matters most.
  • Switch your environment:Take a short walk, move to another room, or study in a new place like a library to reset your focus.
  • Look for a different explanation:Search for an explainer video, read an alternative guide, or ask a peer to explain the concept differently.
